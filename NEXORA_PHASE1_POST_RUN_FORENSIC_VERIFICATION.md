## 9. Check 8: Disclosed Inconsistencies & Minor Observations

The forensic audit identified **zero critical or blocking defects**, and notes the following two minor technical observations:

### Observation 1: Wall-Clock Throughput vs. Active Step Throughput Reporting
- **Detail:** In [`NEXORA_GPT100M_PHASE1_100_STEP_SMOKE_TEST_REPORT.md`](file:///c:/Projects/CUDA-GPU-Acceleration-Engine/NEXORA_GPT100M_PHASE1_100_STEP_SMOKE_TEST_REPORT.md), the executive scorecard reports an average throughput of **$6,343.5\text{ tok/sec}$**, which is the arithmetic mean of the per-step compute throughputs. Over total elapsed wall-clock time ($39.47\text{ s}$), which includes Step 50 and Step 100 validation passes and two checkpoint disk serializations, the effective end-to-end throughput is **$5,188.8\text{ tok/sec}$**.
- **Impact:** Negligible. Both throughputs are far above the platform's required minimum threshold of $1,500\text{ tok/sec}$.
- **Resolution:** Explicitly document both metrics in all future progress reporting to distinguish pure GPU compute throughput from end-to-end elapsed throughput.

### Observation 2: Model Naming ("GPT-100M" vs. 86.8M Parameter Count)
- **Detail:** The model directory is named `flagship_100m_recovery`, but the model instantiates with exactly $86,825,472$ parameters.
- **Explanation:** As established in the forensic architecture report, "GPT-100M" is the project's model tier name. A 12-layer, 12-head, 768-dim transformer parameter count varies with vocabulary size ($V$) and context window ($T$):
  - At $V=512, T=128$ (Pilot): $85,547,520$ parameters
  - At $V=2048, T=256$ (Production 10M Corpus): **$86,825,472$ parameters**
  - At $V=8192, T=1024$ (Full Preset): $92,133,888$ parameters
- **Impact:** No technical defect. $86.8\text{M}$ is the exact mathematical parameter count required for compatibility with the verified 2,048-token production corpus.
